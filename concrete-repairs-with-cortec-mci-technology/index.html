Concrete Repairs with Cortec® MCI® Technology

Rebar corrosion causes cracking, delamination and spalling. Learn what Cortec®'s patented MCI® Migrating Corrosion Inhibitors are, how they reach and protect reinforcement in new and existing concrete, and which MCI® products suit repair work.

MCI® Technology: How Migrating Corrosion Inhibitors Protect Reinforced Concrete

Several physical and chemical forces reduce the strength and soundness of concrete reinforcement, and corrosion is the most significant. As rebar corrodes it expands, creating tensile stress in the surrounding concrete that eventually leads to cracking, delamination and spalling.

Left untreated, minor cracks and chipping can erode concrete durability and accelerate structural deterioration. Engineers therefore need a dependable way to prevent, or at least delay, corrosion of steel reinforcement, and this is where Cortec's industry-leading Migrating Corrosion Inhibitor™ (MCI®) portfolio comes in.

What Is MCI® Technology?

Cortec Corporation holds the patent on its MCI® (Migrating Corrosion Inhibitors) technology, which substantially extends the service life of reinforced concrete. Several corrosion mitigation methods are available, but MCI® is widely chosen for its economics and simple application.

MCI® is a relatively new group of amine-based corrosion inhibitors. It is classed as a mixed inhibitor because it acts on both the anodic and cathodic sides of a corrosion cell. Once applied, it diffuses through the concrete pores and forms a monomolecular film between the rebar and the concrete, delaying the onset and spread of corrosion. It can be used preventively in new structures or as a repair method for existing ones.

How Does Cortec® MCI® Technology Work?

Cortec MCI® can be added as an admixture to fresh concrete at the ready-mix plant or on site, or applied topically to existing structures. After application, it moves through porous concrete by capillary action, vapor diffusion and ionic attraction. On reaching the rebar, it forms a protective molecular layer that prevents corrosive agents from reacting with the metal, greatly extending durability and service life.

Repairing and rehabilitating concrete with Cortec® MCI® adds safety, reliability and functionality, and also improves appearance and water tightness. The following Cortec® MCI® products are effective at preventing corrosion of concrete structures:

  • MCI®-2005
  • MCI®-2020
  • MCI®-309
  • MCI® PTC Emitters
  • MCI® Minigrenades
  • MCI® CorShield®
  • MCI®-2018
  • CorrVerter®
  • MCI®-2061

What Are the Major Benefits of Cortec® MCI® Technology?

Cortec's MCI® technology is a practical, cost-effective way to protect existing concrete from corrosion and extend its service life. As a time-tested option, it is also greener, with lower toxicity and environmental impact than traditional corrosion-inhibiting admixtures. It is made from renewable resources, specifically amine alcohols and amine carboxylates, which helps structures meet sustainability standards.

Only small quantities of these inhibitors are needed, and the required dosage does not depend on chloride levels. Cortec® MCI® products are UL certified for compliance with NSF/ANSI Standard 61 for potable water structures. Using them for concrete repair improves the durability, integrity and service life of existing reinforced structures. They can be applied by spray, brush or roller and need only minimal curing time, making them well suited to repairing and rehabilitating reinforced concrete.
