Aboveground storage tanks

Problem

Soil-side corrosion is a leading cause of tank failure. Asphalt pads, bituminous sand, cathodic protection and coatings — alone or combined — often fail to stop pitting, because air gaps under the bottom plates shield areas from CP current.

  • Unscheduled interruptions
  • Loss of product
  • Costly repairs and safety concerns
Solution

The CorroLogic® System for AST uses amine-carboxylate Vapor phase Corrosion Inhibitors that volatilise and adsorb onto the bottom plate, reaching hard-to-access areas and supplementing CP. More than 600 tanks are protected today — new, out-of-service and in-service.

Benefit
  • Reduces pitting corrosion and metal-loss rate
  • Extends inspection intervals and bottom-plate life
  • Monitors corrosivity under the tank
  • Compatible with cathodic protection

01 — VpCI® technology

Vapor phase protection that reaches everywhere.

VCI is a general term for volatile, vapour or vapor phase corrosion inhibitors — technology that protects metal in enclosed spaces such as packages, equipment internals and structural cavities. Cortec® trademarks its VCIs as VpCI®.

How does it work?

VpCI® molecules line up parallel to each other and perpendicular to the metal surface, forming a mono-molecular layer. This layer displaces water and protects both the anodic and cathodic parts of the corrosion cell.

Facts about VCIs

  • Spread from high to low concentration, reaching every part of an enclosure
  • Go anywhere oxygen can — crevices and hard-to-reach spots
  • Attracted to metal, where they condense to protect
  • Amine-carboxylate dipoles protecting multiple metals in electrical, static, rotating and civil equipment
How VpCI® technology works
01

Less labour, less waste

No special removal of coatings or greasy rust preventatives — reducing labour time and hazardous-waste disposal compared with petroleum-based preventatives and solvent cleaners.

02

No power needed

Unlike dehumidification systems, VpCI® needs no constant electricity supply — and protects intricate internals such as pipes, boilers and valves where coatings are hard to apply.

03

Three-phase, multi-metal

Protects multiple metal types in the liquid, vapour and interface phases — above and below the fluid level. Many products are recyclable or water-based and safer than nitrite-based VCIs.

02 — MCI® technology

Migrating Corrosion Inhibitors — from grey to green.

Cortec's patented MCI® technology protects reinforcing metal in concrete. It greatly extends the service life of new and existing structures by delaying the onset of corrosion and keeping corrosion rates low once it has started.

MCI® for concrete

MCIs are amine-based (amine alcohols and amine carboxylates) mixed inhibitors that act on both the anodic and cathodic portions of a corrosion cell.

MCI® can be added as a concrete admixture or applied as a topical treatment. It migrates as a liquid by capillary action and as a vapour through the pore structure. On contact with embedded metal it forms a protective molecular layer that blocks further corrosive reactions and reduces existing corrosion rates.

  • Admixtures for new construction
  • Surface treatments for existing structures
  • Repair mortars and concrete repair systems

How we handle your data.

Personal data

“Personal data” means any information relating to an identified or identifiable individual. When you request services or information, or contact us directly, we may ask for contact details such as your name, company, address, email address and phone number. We use this information to respond to you and to market our products and services.

Testimonials

We may publish customers' names and job titles in a list of customers and testimonials. We always obtain the customer's authorisation before publishing any testimonial.

Use of information

We never sell, share or rent your data to third parties, and do not use it for purposes other than those described in this policy.

Analytics

Like most websites, we automatically gather certain information — such as IP address, browser type and operating system, internet service provider, referring and exit pages, date and time stamps and clickstream data — to analyse trends and administer the site. We use analytics providers such as Google Analytics, which uses cookies to collect non-identifying information. We do not link this data to personal information.

Cookies & tracking

Cookies are small text files placed in your browser. We and our partners use cookies, pixel tags (web beacons) and similar technologies to analyse trends, administer the site and understand how visitors use it. You can control cookies in your browser settings; if you reject them you can still use the site, though some features may be limited.

Advertising

We may partner with third parties to display or manage advertising, which may use cookies to show ads based on your interests. You can opt out at aboutads.info/choices and manage Google ad preferences at google.com/ads/preferences.

Third-party links

Our site links to other websites whose privacy practices may differ from ours. Information you submit to those sites is governed by their policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time; the current version is always available on this page. Continued use of the site means you accept the changes. Questions? Email inquiries@cortec-me.com.
